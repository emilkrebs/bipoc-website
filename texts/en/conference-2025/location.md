This year we will be located in Basque Country in **Northern Spain** about an hour away from Bilbao. We are teaming up with a beautiful venue in a rural area. Due to security precautions, we will not share the explicit location of the venue on the website.  

Once the registrations are approved, we will share all relevant details with attendees and contributors.

We do not expect any kind of incident to occur! Besides that, we are planning a safety strategy for transport to and from the venue.
