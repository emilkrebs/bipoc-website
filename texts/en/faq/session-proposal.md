---
title: "I want to propose a session, but I can't find anything related in the program draft. Can I still submit it?"
---

Yes! Our program draft is a curation that we came up with as a roadmap to move
towards our overall objective of the conference. This is a tool that should help
and inspire us. It is not a tool of exclusion. All proposals are welcome! We
will do our best to fit all the ideas into the program.
